## Agency overview
TechAbout designs, builds and operates software for organisations that need more than a prototype. We cover enterprise software, web and mobile apps, AI automation, cloud and DevOps, data engineering and cybersecurity, and we stay involved after launch with documentation and operational handover.

Our offices are in Lahore, Sheridan (USA) and Dubai (UAE). For smaller, well-defined jobs we offer Express services, including website migration, WordPress to Next.js conversion, store setup and cross-platform mobile apps.
