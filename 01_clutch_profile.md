## Company overview (about 150 words)
TechAbout is a technology company with offices in Lahore, Sheridan (Wyoming) and Dubai. We help organisations plan, build and run digital products, from enterprise software and web and mobile applications to cloud infrastructure, data pipelines and AI automation.

Each engagement starts with a clear statement of the problem and constraints. Scope, ownership and delivery terms are agreed before work begins, and teams can engage us for discovery only, a defined build, or ongoing operation.

Our work spans six core areas: AI and machine learning, enterprise software development, cloud infrastructure and DevOps, web and mobile app development, data engineering and analytics, and cybersecurity and compliance. We also offer fixed-scope Express services such as website migration and cross-platform mobile app development.

Alongside client work, we build and operate our own products, including Travel.pk and PKHosting.com.
